About us

Built around the scope, equipped for the whole hospital.

Ikeda Endoscopy Africa supplies and supports medical equipment for hospitals and clinics, with laparoscopy for surgery, gynecology and urology as our core specialty.

Overview

What we do

We supply and support medical equipment across a wide range of hospital departments. Our specialty is laparoscopy for surgery, gynecology and urology, and we back that expertise with a full multi-specialty catalog spanning neonatology, maternity, intensive care, ophthalmology, dentistry, orthopedics, gastroenterology, pediatrics, radiology, emergency care, ENT and physiotherapy.

Every product line is sourced from established manufacturers and backed by local technical support, from installation to after-sales service.

Mission

Our mission

To equip healthcare providers across Africa with reliable, high-quality medical equipment and hands-on technical expertise — anchored in advanced laparoscopic solutions for surgery, gynecology and urology — so that every facility we serve can offer safer, more precise care.

Vision

Our vision

To be East Africa's most trusted multi-specialty medical equipment partner — recognized for technical expertise, dependable supply, and support that lasts long after installation.

What sets us apart

Specialist depth

Laparoscopy is our core discipline — we know the equipment and the procedures it's built for.

Broad coverage

Sixteen departments under one supplier, so equipping a facility doesn't mean juggling a dozen vendors.

Local support

Installation, training and after-sales service, close to where our equipment is used.
