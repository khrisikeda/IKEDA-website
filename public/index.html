Our Flagship Specialty

Laparoscopy for Surgery, Gynecology & Urology

Minimally invasive procedures require exceptional optical clarity, smooth ergonomics, and unwavering equipment reliability. We supply complete laparoscopic operating suites engineered for low-latency visualization and precision intervention.

  • General Surgery: 4K/Full HD video towers, LED cold light sources, and automated 30–45L/min CO2 insufflation.
  • Gynecology: Continuous-flow diagnostic & operative hysteroscopes, LEEP units, and video colposcopes.
  • Urology & Endourology: Continuous-flow resectoscopes, semi-rigid ureterorenoscopes, and stone management tools.
  • Local Biomedical Engineering: Turnkey installation, clinical staff training, and preventative servicing.
