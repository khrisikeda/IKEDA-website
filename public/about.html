Who We Are

Built around the scope. Dedicated to Africa's healthcare facilities.

IKEDA ENDOSCOPY AFRICA Ltd was established to bridge the gap between world-class medical instrumentation and clinical care. We combine advanced minimally invasive optical systems with comprehensive multi-department hospital equipment.

Company Profile

Elevating Surgical & Diagnostic Standards

Headquartered in Kigali (Kicukiro, Masaka, Cyimo, Kabeza), Rwanda, IKEDA ENDOSCOPY AFRICA Ltd works hand-in-hand with regional hospitals, specialized surgical centers, mission facilities, and referral institutions.

While minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery, gynecology, and urology form our flagship clinical specialty, our full equipment catalog spans 16 core departments — from high-frequency neonatal incubators and ICU ventilators to digital radiography and physiotherapy suites.

Crucially, we do not simply supply boxed equipment: our in-house biomedical engineering team handles physical installation, calibration, safety compliance testing, and continuous staff clinical training.

Our Mission

Empowering Healthcare

To equip healthcare providers across Rwanda and Africa with reliable, high-grade medical technology and responsive technical expertise — anchored in advanced laparoscopic solutions for surgery, gynecology, and urology — so that every facility we serve can perform safer, minimally invasive care with confidence.

Our Vision

Regional Leadership & Trust

To be East and Central Africa's most dependable and trusted multi-specialty medical technology partner — recognized for clinical depth, authentic equipment supply, and technical maintenance that lasts throughout the lifecycle of every instrument.
