Company Profile

Elevating Surgical & Diagnostic Standards

IKEDA Endoscopy Africa Ltd, founded in March 2025 and based in Masaka (opposite IRCAD AFRICA), Kigali, Rwanda, specializes in medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, and surgical technologies. We are committed to delivering reliable, carefully selected medical products that support healthcare safety, clinical effectiveness, and improved medical healthcare outcomes.

Backed by our professional and dedicated team, IKEDA provides comprehensive after-sales service, technical support, and training programs, ensuring safe, efficient product use and long-term service assurance for our customers.

Our strength lies in our in-depth understanding of the technical background of medical device manufacturers. Based on this expertise, we are able to carefully select high-quality brands for you and act as your trusted gatekeeper. We are committed to minimizing potential risks and helping you avoid unnecessary pitfalls, ensuring that your investment in medical equipment delivers the safest and most cost-effective returns.

Our Mission

Empowering Healthcare

To equip healthcare providers across Rwanda and Africa with reliable, high-grade medical technology and responsive technical expertise — anchored in advanced laparoscopic solutions for surgery, gynecology, and urology — so that every facility we serve can perform safer, minimally invasive care with confidence.

Our Vision

Regional Leadership & Trust

To be East and Central Africa's most dependable and trusted multi-specialty medical technology partner — recognized for clinical depth, authentic equipment supply, and technical maintenance that lasts throughout the lifecycle of every instrument.
